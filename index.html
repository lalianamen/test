Three Levels of Integration

All three send applications to AutoPay under your partner channel — the difference is how deeply the process lives inside your site

Level 1

Button

$350

A “Get Approved” button in your menu and on key pages. The customer clicks and goes to the AutoPay application page, set up under your partner program.

The application is completed on AutoPay's side. Your website plays no part in handling customer data at all.

Upside: live in a day or two, works immediately.

Trade-off: the customer leaves your site — some drop off at that step.

Level 2

Embedded form

$650–1,200

The form sits directly on your page, inside your design. The customer fills it out without ever leaving myautolux.com.

Technically it is a secure AutoPay window inside your page: customer data — including the SSN — goes straight to AutoPay, bypassing your website entirely. Your server never sees it and never stores it. Responsibility for personal data stays fully with the financing partner, exactly as it does today.

Upside: the customer stays with you, higher conversion, zero data liability.

Trade-off: the form's appearance is set by AutoPay — styling options are limited.

Level 3

Live rates

$3,000–6,000

Your own quote form on your site. The customer enters their details and within seconds sees real offers: interest rate, term, monthly payment, down payment. They pick one and move on to the application.

Here the data passes through a secure service module built and maintained specifically for you: it holds the partner access keys and forwards information to AutoPay in transit, storing nothing. Requires separate approval from The Savings Group and partner API keys.

Upside: full control over look and logic — the quote reads as your own service.

Trade-off: longest to launch, plus a small monthly cost to run the module (about $0–20).

My recommendation: start with Level 1 — the button pays for itself on the first deal and launches right away. Once applications start coming in and you can see the real click-through numbers, move to Level 2: a form on your own site, with the same zero data obligations, gives a noticeable lift in submissions. Level 3 makes sense when the flow is steady and you want the financing quote to become part of the AutoLux brand itself.

In all three scenarios the SSN and credit information are stored only by AutoPay. Your website never stores them under any option — what changes is simply where the customer physically fills out the form.
